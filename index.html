    Renting from abroad is risky

    Spanish National Police and INCIBE (National Cybersecurity Institute) warn against recurring Madrid rental traps.

    • The photos are stolen

      Scammers duplicate real listings and photos, then offer the flat at a price that is hard to ignore to create urgency.

    • Payment demanded before a visit

      You are pressured into paying a deposit or reservation fee before anyone shows you the flat, often via wire transfer outside secure platforms.

    • The owner is conveniently absent

      The landlord claims to be living abroad or says a key courier service will deliver access. Nobody can meet you, and nobody can prove legal ownership.

    What we check

    Five critical audit pillars recorded in your report as Verified, Flagged, or Failed.

    • Property Registry (*Nota Simple*)

      We verify the registered owner using the official land registry extract (*nota simple*) to ensure the person offering the flat has legal title to rent it.

    • Identity & Owner Match

      We verify government ID against land registry records and conduct in-person or live video verification inside the property.

    • Listing Authenticity & Price

      We perform reverse image searches to detect stolen photos and benchmark rent rates against neighborhood averages.

    • Physical Apartment Audit

      We inspect hot water, heating/AC, appliances, electrics, measured Wi-Fi speeds, dampness, and street noise levels on-site.

    • Contract Clauses & Padrón

      We review lease conditions for abusive terms or illegal fee clauses and verify written landlord commitment for municipal residence registration (*empadronamiento*).

    Scope & Legal Disclaimer

    A report reflects conditions verified on the date of inspection. It provides objective findings and recommendations but does not constitute formal legal counsel. For complex legal disputes, consult a qualified Spanish lawyer.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Do you list or broker rental properties?

    No. We operate strictly as an independent verification layer. You find the apartments on public portals, and we verify them so there is zero conflict of interest.

    Can you guarantee an apartment is 100% risk-free?

    No company can legally guarantee future landlord behavior. A FlatCheck report provides verified facts, official land registry data, physical inspection findings, and an objective risk assessment.

    How do you verify property ownership?

    We verify government identity documents against the official Property Registry extract (*nota simple*), which confirms legal ownership or authorized management representation.

    Do you inspect every flat in person?

    With "Verify One", yes. With "Shortlist", we perform comprehensive remote screening first, then visit the top 3 verified properties in person.

    Which areas in Madrid do you cover?

    We cover all central Madrid neighborhoods (Centro, Chamberí, Salamanca, Moncloa, Retiro, Arganzuela, Tetuán, etc.) and major university suburb campuses.

    How long does a verification take?

    Remote screening takes 24 hours. Physical in-person visits are typically completed within 24 to 48 hours depending on landlord availability.
