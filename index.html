Got questions?

How do you check that the owner is real?

We match three names: the owner on the official Land Registry (Nota Simple), the ID shown live on video, and the bank account holder. If they match, it's real.

Who visits the flat?

A verified IE student, trained with our standard checklist. Every visit comes with time-stamped photos and video, so you can see the proof yourself. Checkers are paid per check, never by landlords, so they have no reason to say yes to a bad flat.

Do you rent or sell apartments?

No. Right now we only check flats that you find. You pay us, not the landlord, so we work for you.

Why can't I just trust the photos online?

Photos can be old, edited, or copied from another flat. Some listings aren't real at all. We go in person, so you know what's actually there.

How long does a check take?

We aim to do the fast check within a few hours, and the visit within 24–48 hours, depending on when the landlord can show the flat.

Can you guarantee I'll get the flat?

No. The landlord decides who rents. We make sure the flat and owner are real, so you can decide with confidence.

Do you help with the Empadronamiento (City Registration)?

Yes, as guidance. We share the steps, tips and documents you need. We can't do the registration for you.

How much does it cost and how do I pay?

€49 per flat, paid when you book. Full refund if we can't arrange a check.

What if the landlord won't let you visit?

If we can't arrange a visit, you get a full refund. A landlord who refuses a visit is also a warning sign.

What if you find a problem with the flat?

We tell you clearly in your report, with a "No-Go" and the reasons, so you can walk away before paying anything.

What do you do with my personal data?

We use it only to check your flat and delete it after your rental is done. Email shiv2140@student.ie.edu to have it deleted anytime.
