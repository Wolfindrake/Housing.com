Got questions?

Do you rent or sell apartments?

No. We don't list, rent or sell flats, and we take no commission from landlords. We work only for you.

Why can't I just trust the photos online?

Photos can be old, edited, or copied from another flat. Some listings aren't real at all. We go in person, so you know what's actually there.

How long does a check take?

We aim to send your report within 48 hours of booking. It depends on when the landlord can show the flat.

Can you guarantee I'll get the flat?

No. The landlord decides who rents. We make sure the flat and owner are real, so you can decide with confidence.

Do you help with the Empadronamiento (City Registration)?

Yes, as guidance. We share the steps, tips and documents you need. We can't do the registration for you.

How much does it cost and how do I pay?

The first 5 students get their check free. After that, it's €49 per flat, paid when you book.

What if the landlord won't let you visit?

If we can't arrange a visit, you get a full refund. A landlord who refuses a visit is also a warning sign.

What if you find a problem with the flat?

We tell you clearly in your report, with a "No-Go" and the reasons, so you don't lose your deposit.

How do you check that the owner is real?

We compare the landlord's ID with the official property registry (nota simple) to confirm they own the flat.

What do you do with my personal data?

We use it only to check your flat and delete it after your rental is done. Email shiv2140@student.ie.edu to have it deleted anytime.

Terms & Conditions

  • Payments & Refunds: Payment is required before the flat visit. If we cannot arrange a visit with the landlord or agency, you will receive a full refund.
  • Timelines: We aim to deliver reports within 48 hours, but this is strictly dependent on landlord availability.
  • Scope of Service: We verify the property and report conditions at the time of the visit. We do not act as a broker, and we cannot guarantee you will secure the flat.
  • Empadronamiento: We provide guidance and steps for municipal registration. We do not process or submit the registration on your behalf.
  • Legal Advice: We review leases to flag unclear terms, but we do not provide formal legal advice.

Privacy Policy (GDPR)

We collect your name, email, move-in preferences, and target properties strictly to provide our verification service. We do not sell your data. We retain your information only until your rental process is complete, after which it is deleted. To request immediate deletion of your data, email us at shiv2140@student.ie.edu.
